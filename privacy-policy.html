Asteroid Smashers

Privacy Policy

Effective 20 November 2026

This policy explains how Asteroid Smashers handles information. The game is an offline arcade experience, and progress stays on this device. The Android package name is com.w3stech.asteroidsmeshars.

Information stored on this device

The game saves the following in private app storage on your device:

  • Best score, credits, crystals, and run history
  • Ship, weapon, upgrade, mission, and cosmetic progress
  • Audio, graphics, control, and accessibility settings

This information is not sent to a server by the game.

Information we do not collect

Asteroid Smashers does not ask you to create an account. The game itself does not ask for your name, email address, location, or contacts, and it does not sell personal information. Advertising data handled by Google is described below.

Advertising

A banner from Google AdMob may appear at the bottom of the screen and change to a different placement about every 45 seconds. When a run ends, a full-screen ad may open, using one placement chosen at random. You dismiss that screen with the close control on the ad. Google may process device information and the advertising identifier to show and measure ads, under Google's policies at https://policies.google.com/privacy. The game does not sell that information.

Permissions

The app uses internet and network-state access so banner ads can load. It also uses vibration for optional haptic feedback during play. You can turn haptics off in Settings.

Device backup

Android backup and device transfer may include the game save stored on this device, according to your device backup settings.

Children

Asteroid Smashers is a general-audience arcade game. It is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

How long information stays

Saved progress and settings remain on the device until you reset them in Settings, clear the app storage, or uninstall the game.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated text will appear in the game and on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer at tutorialspointexamples@gmail.com.
